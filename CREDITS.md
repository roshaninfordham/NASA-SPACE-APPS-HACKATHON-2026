# Red Veil credits and asset provenance

## Runtime

Godot Engine 4.6.3, MIT license. Official export templates are verified against the publisher's SHA-512 manifest. Notices are pinned to the engine’s `4.6.3-stable` source tag. Engine and third-party notices are bundled in `game/licenses/Godot-MIT.txt` and `Godot-THIRDPARTY.txt`.

## Kenney starter kit

Source: https://github.com/KenneyNL/Starter-Kit-3D-Platformer

Inspected revision: `3fa8a04b1c01ab23db43123d4ce814a34c3fc7f0`.

The repository's code is MIT licensed; its README dedicates included 2D/3D assets and sound effects to CC0. Camera-relative controller patterns informed the independently adapted first-person controller. Selected `coin`, `land`, `walking`, and `jump` OGG files are included unchanged. The full MIT notice is in `game/licenses/Kenney-MIT.txt`.

## Font

Lilita One by Juan Montoreano, SIL Open Font License 1.1. The bundled font is unmodified. Its complete notice is in `game/licenses/Lilita-OFL.txt`. It is provisional and replaceable by the design team.

## Science

NASA public publications supply the numerical observations identified in `data/nasa-observations.json` and `docs/SCIENCE.md`. NASA does not endorse this project. No NASA insignia is used as a product logo. Publication summaries are not raw instrument datasets, and the game's hardware parameters and emergency timeline are illustrative.

## Project visuals

Current outpost geometry, interface arrangement, icon, and scanner are provisional procedural project work. They do not reproduce reference-document images. Future third-party art must add its creator, license, source revision, and modification record here before distribution.
