**Historical 0.10 contract · November 15, 2026.** This records the published 0.10 interface. The [0.11 candidate contract](JOURNEY-0.11.md) describes the restoration now in progress; it does not alter this release’s screenshots or acceptance record. This simplifies the [0.9 presentation](JOURNEY-0.9.md) without changing the [mission equations](../TRAINING-MODEL.md). Publication and physical-device acceptance are separate from implementation.

## First action, then optional detail

The intended player is 7–12, inside mission control. They need to find Start, see what pace changes, reach an ending and explain a connection. Three short illustrated story scenes introduce Mars, Ren and the control room. Actual first-scene preparation shows **Opening the window…**, with **Back to Home** cancellation and no invented progress percentage.

Preflight freezes every model quantity until explicit Start. The primary view contains **panel cleaned**, **energy stored** and **storm time**, plus one shared pace control: a red seven-step slider and Slower/Faster buttons (keys 1/3). Ren's brush and the panel remain visible. A brief effort description replaces dense fatigue/rate telemetry in the main view.

The timer is real play time: about 90 active seconds at standard pacing. **Time → Learn more** explains the 30 simulated minutes used by the equations. Reading, pause and focus loss freeze the whole model; accessibility clock settings change real duration without changing its numerical rules.

## Eight concepts remain available

**Science** opens a paused discovery library with eight actual-state concept tiles. Each card first shows a short lead, diagram, everyday analogy and concise current reading. **Learn more** reveals the original detailed explanation and equation. **Data & sources** exposes the evidence, assumptions and source links separately. No reading percentage gates play, and opening a card does not prove mastery.

Closing a card restores its caller. The library returns to the mission; paused mission/navigation layers preserve their state. The optional NASA viewer remains separate observational context, with honest online/cached/fallback labels; its imagery never changes Mars dose or score.

## Result first, then relationships

Ren enters automatically at the fixed deadline. WIN/PASS/TRY AGAIN still uses cleaned fraction alone: at least 80%, at least 40%, or below 40%. The result shows actual cleaned percentage, stored kWh and absorbed model µGy, with no health-dose progress bar. Every outcome places Ren inside.

**See how science connects** merges the eight ideas into four short relationship cards:

1. Average effective cleaning rate × cleaning time → cleaned panel.
2. Less dust → more solar power.
3. Collected energy − used energy → battery change, with starting energy and capacity explained.
4. Average radiation dose rate × outside time → absorbed model dose.

**Explore my numbers and graphs** reveals the full precise word equations, numerical values and history. Shelter and sievert remain accessible context concepts. Back to results, Try another plan and Home have explicit destinations; reduced motion reaches the same material without the animated delay.

## Feedback, evidence and delivery

Original softer pace, card and discovery sounds complement visible feedback. Alert cues take priority over optional sounds; cooldowns limit repetition. Mute, pause, focus loss and reduced-feedback preferences remain respected. Existing music credits are unchanged, and the unavailable Drive recording is not represented as shipped.

The 0.10 source gallery covers 135 states at 1280×720, 960×540, 390×844, 320×568 and 844×390. Record source hashes, fixtures and renderer; keep old media immutable. Native source navigation, layout tests, exported-package startup, signed-content updates, physical-device performance and supervised child usability have different evidence scopes.

The installation guide must describe the exact versioned package and real app names. The next release preserves save/update identities and requires the six-baseline Mac/Windows matrix, Linux 0.4 gate, exact PDF inclusion and public-file verification.
