The new window barrier and door phases establish scene geometry and state transitions. They do **not** add gas/pressure dynamics, radiation transport or a certified shielded habitat. The cycle is a labelled scenario sequence. Earlier outdoor-EVA collision and inspection behavior remains separately testable when its presentation is restored.

## Review the current direction

1. Start the default mission. The forward camera should show dunes and Mira through clear glazing, with no exterior structures or handheld scanner intruding. Turning should reveal a real room and side entry.
2. Check waiting, walking, cycling, entering and protected states. Mira must use the side portal, not cross the central window. Both doors remain closed during cycling; shielding begins only after indoor arrival.
3. Pause or open the planner during travel. Mission time and Mira’s movement must freeze together; return restores control. An abort must not later complete an already halted rescue.
4. Verify that base energy, Mira’s suit/dose and warnings use actual state. Planner results must identify their frozen-protection assumption and distinguish an aborted short run from a full-horizon outcome.
5. Check three primary choices/actions and optional learning at desktop, portrait and landscape sizes. Restore the legacy outdoor presentation and confirm its routes/colliders remain usable.
6. Record final-source screenshots and native/browser acceptance separately from headless tests. Do not reuse the earlier exterior-start audit as proof of this new default flow.

## Remaining production work

Final authored character/environment assets, a richer rescue curriculum, validated life-support/thermal/pressure models, original licensed audio, full accessibility, physical-device performance and supervised learning studies remain open. The source documents’ six-/seven-sol campaigns, construction game, upgrade tree, independent water/oxygen loop and three new EVA scenarios were not delivered by renaming or simplifying the interface.

Future work should proceed through the [design contracts](DESIGN-HANDOFF.md), [roadmap](ROADMAP.md) and evidence-based release process. Later source changes require their own release evidence. A visually cleaner scene is not automatically a more effective lesson; expert review, physical-device studies and supervised learner evaluation remain open.
