# <!-- brand:display_name -->KŌMAKU: Shield Protocol<!-- /brand --> playtest protocol

Status: proposed protocol; no participants or results yet.

## Audience and safeguards

Primary audience: ages 7–12. Use adult-supervised sessions, appropriate guardian permission, and the organizer's child-participation rules. Collect no names, contact information, photos, voice recordings, or behavioral telemetry by default. The game itself has no analytics. Keep any facilitator notes anonymous and local unless a separate, appropriate data-handling process is established.

## Questions to answer

- Can the learner identify their first task and navigate to it?
- Can they distinguish the visor battery from the habitat battery?
- Do they discover why panel cleaning helps less during atmospheric darkness?
- Can they recognize the shelter cue and get inside without frustration?
- Do they want to try a different plan, and can they explain why?

## Ten-minute session

1. Ask the learner what they expect a dusty sky to do to solar panels.
2. Let them start the mission with minimal explanation. Note where they ask for help.
3. After the debrief, ask them to describe one choice they would change.
4. Offer a second attempt without pressure. Observe whether the plan changes.
5. Ask: “If the panel is clean but the sky is full of dust, what happens?” and “Which battery powers your visor?”

## Record observable outcomes

Record completion/abort reason, navigation difficulty, prompts needing explanation, whether the learner distinguishes the two dust mechanisms, and whether they can name a useful strategy change. Treat age bands separately. Do not use raw survival as proof of learning; a player can succeed by following instructions without understanding them.

## Device check for the primary MacBook

Record model, macOS version, release version, display resolution, input method, first-launch behavior, frame pacing, process memory, and noticeable heat or battery impact. Repeat after ten mission restarts. A successful launch on one Mac does not establish all-device support.
