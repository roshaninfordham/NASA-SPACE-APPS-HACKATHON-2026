# <!-- brand:display_name -->KŌMAKU: Dust & Radiation<!-- /brand --> delivery roadmap

**Current source/package: 0.10.0-alpha.1; publication pending.** This interface update simplifies reading and navigation and corrects the installation handoff; the mission equations remain unchanged from the published [0.9.0-alpha.1](https://github.com/roshaninfordham/NASA-SPACE-APPS-HACKATHON-2026/releases/tag/v0.9.0-alpha.1). The 0.9.0 release was published November 15, 2026 at 13:12:47 UTC, and its assets remain unchanged. [Release notes](releases/0.10.0-alpha.1.md).

The immediate goal is **a readable opening → close window mission → 90-second round → clear result → four science relationships**. Keep the player indoors with Ren and the panel close to the window. Three main readings, one pace control and optional science details support the action; restrained Asian-inspired details remain, while older ornate dome references remain design history.

The current release acceptance does not by itself establish physical M5 performance, all mobile/browser support, learning effectiveness, photoreal reference fidelity or a complete astronaut curriculum. Those claims need their own measurements and reviews.

After the release, priority is feedback from the design team and target learners: can a child find Start, see what speed changes, reach an ending, and explain one connection? Use that evidence to guide the next mission and any future personalized progression.
