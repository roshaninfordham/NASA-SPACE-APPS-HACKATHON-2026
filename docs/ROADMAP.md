# <!-- brand:display_name -->KŌMAKU: Dust & Radiation<!-- /brand --> delivery roadmap

**Current source candidate: 0.9.0-alpha.1; not yet published.** [0.8.0-alpha.1](https://github.com/roshaninfordham/NASA-SPACE-APPS-HACKATHON-2026/releases/tag/v0.8.0-alpha.1) remains the published baseline with [verified assets](evidence/release-0.8.0-alpha.1.json).

The immediate goal is **a readable opening → close window mission → 90-second round → clear result → four science relationships**. Keep the player indoors with Ren and the panel close to the window. Larger controls and restrained Asian-inspired details support the action; older ornate dome references remain design history.

| #   | Outcome                                 | Current candidate work / established baseline                                                                   | Next acceptance gate                                                                                     |

The current release acceptance does not by itself establish physical M5 performance, all mobile/browser support, learning effectiveness, photoreal reference fidelity or a complete astronaut curriculum. Those claims need their own measurements and reviews.

After the release, priority is feedback from the design team and target learners: can a child find Start, see what speed changes, reach an ending, and explain one connection? Use that evidence to guide the next mission and any future personalized progression.
