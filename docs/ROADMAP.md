The 0.7.1 patch defers hidden optional world construction while retaining the default window framing. [Startup evidence](WINDOW-STARTUP-PERFORMANCE.md) records the lower initial cost and the first-use/cache tradeoff; these results are not device FPS guarantees.

The next product gates are continued design review, physical-device testing, expert science review and supervised learning evaluation. Completing the ten delivery outcomes does not mean that the full curriculum, final art, expanded rescue campaign or every platform is finished.

Parallel work uses explicit file ownership and serialized integration. Contribution counts reflect meaningful work; empty commits are not a delivery goal.
