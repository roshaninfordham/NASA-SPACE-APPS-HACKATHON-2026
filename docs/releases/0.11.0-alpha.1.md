# KŌMAKU: Dust & Radiation — 0.11.0-alpha.1

This release restores the view from inside the outpost: Ren cleans the nearby panel through a clearly framed window, with eight live science bubbles alongside it. The larger fonts, short explanations, red pace controls and 90-second mission from 0.10 remain.

## What changes for a player

- **Science window by default.** Radiation, microgray, sievert, dust, energy, time, cleaning pace and shelter remain visible during play. On small screens the bubble rail scrolls horizontally and follows keyboard focus. Selecting a bubble pauses the mission and opens its explanation.
- **Choose your view.** View offers the Science window or a Focused window. Both preserve the mission, camera geometry and science rules. The Focused window keeps the Science button available.
- **Clear navigation and comfort settings.** Menu provides Resume, view/accessibility, Science, sound/motion/round length, Restart and Home. Settings opened from View returns there. Escape handles one layer; its first press in fullscreen returns to a window and pauses. Leaving an active attempt still requires confirmation.
- **Radio feedback you can read.** Pace changes produce a short player caption followed by Ren’s reply, with a brief bubble response. No microphone is needed. Captions work with sound muted; pause and reduced motion are respected.
- **Saved preferences remain local.** Existing profiles migrate without rewriting their files on load. View choices are saved on the next normal save; explored ideas and run history remain intact.

The numerical model is unchanged. Ren enters automatically after 30 simulated minutes. WIN requires at least 80% cleaning, PASS at least 40%, otherwise TRY AGAIN. Energy and absorbed model dose explain the run; they are not extra success conditions or personal-health readings. Every completed default run has the same 10.9375 µGy model dose.

## Downloads and updates

Download the platform ZIP from this release’s **Assets**, extract it completely and follow `START-HERE.txt`. Mac: `KŌMAKU- Dust and Radiation.app`; Windows: `KOMAKU-Dust-and-Radiation.exe`; Linux: `KOMAKU-Dust-and-Radiation.x86_64`. The package includes the current installation guide and three verified science/equation PDFs.

Compatible desktop installations can receive the signed content through **Update available → Install → Restart**. The installed app’s older filename may remain. Engine changes require a full installer. Mac remains ad-hoc signed without Apple notarization; Windows remains unsigned. The existing Mac installer helper is included for trusted downloads. No native Android or iOS installer is claimed.
