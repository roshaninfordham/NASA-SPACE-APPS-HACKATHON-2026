# KŌMAKU: Dust & Radiation — 0.9.0-alpha.1

**Candidate notes: not yet published.** These describe the source prepared for the next release. Native builds, update transitions and public-file verification must pass before these notes become a published release announcement.

The mission is easier to see and quicker to finish: Ren and the solar panel sit closer to the window, controls and science bubbles are larger, and the standard round ends in about **90 active real seconds**. The large timer now shows play time; the smaller timer explains the 30 simulated minutes represented by the lesson.

## What changes

- **New title:** KŌMAKU: Dust & Radiation. Shared branding updates the game and new package names while preserving saves, update trust and app IDs.
- **Clearer work:** a closer view of Ren, the brush and the dusty panel; more visible brushing cadence changes; larger bold labels and a red stepped pace slider.
- **Shorter round:** 90 seconds at the standard 20× clock instead of five active minutes. The simulated equations, fatigue, energy calculations and outcome thresholds remain unchanged.
- **Three story scenes:** exterior habitat, astronaut and interior. Unlettered generated adaptations of the team's references carry separately rendered, larger game titles and story text.
- **A result you can reach:** actual panel-cleaning percentage, stored energy and model dose appear at the deadline. Ren automatically enters the shelter in every outcome.
- **Science after play:** **See how science connects** brings eight bubbles into four equation groups. **Back to results**, **Try another plan** and **Home** make the ending easy to revisit and record. The equations correct the reference artwork's dust/power multiplier and use average effective rates when values change.
- **Warning sounds:** original countdown, warning and urgent beeps with shared rate limiting and pause/focus/mute support. These are a fallback; the mentioned Drive recording was not accessible.
- **Three included PDFs:** the 16-page science guide, two-page technical equations and one-page word-only equations, regenerated from current source and branding.
- **Team handoff:** [submission-writing draft](https://github.com/roshaninfordham/NASA-SPACE-APPS-HACKATHON-2026/blob/main/docs/SUBMISSION-DRAFT.md) and [video/recording guide](https://github.com/roshaninfordham/NASA-SPACE-APPS-HACKATHON-2026/blob/main/docs/VIDEO-PITCH.md) for review. ## Playing and recording

Choose a pace in preflight, then press **Start Mission**. Use **1/SLOWER**, **3/FASTER**, or the slider. Reading a bubble or pausing freezes the round. Escape handles the current dialog/pause/fullscreen state; F11 toggles fullscreen.

At the ending, select **See how science connects** to record the bubble merge and equations. A 30-second pitch needs edited highlights from the full 90-second round; retain the actual readings rather than overlaying example numbers from a design image.

WIN is at least 80% cleaned, PASS at least 40%, and TRY AGAIN below 40%. All three end with Ren inside. Energy and dose explain the run; they are not extra hidden win conditions. Every completed default round has the same 10.9375 µGy model dose because its outside time and authored radiation curve are fixed.

## Science and asset notes

Curiosity's historical RAD measurements provide the starting absorbed-dose reference. Pathfinder's 0.28% per-day result is a decrease in solar-cell short-circuit current, not a universal power-loss coefficient. The optional NASA SDO image view remains separate from the Mars equations and score. Cleaning, tiredness, hardware and the approaching storm are labelled mission assumptions.

Story art is generated adaptation, not an exact recovery of the user's inline images or a real-time gameplay render. The original warning cues and existing music have separate provenance/credits. Physical-device performance, expert science review and learning studies remain distinct from code and hosted-runner checks.
